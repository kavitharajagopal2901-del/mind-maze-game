An adaptive brain-challenge game

Find your way out of the maze.

Three levels, nine locked doors, one exit. Each door is a puzzle: logic, patterns, memory, codes or maths. Answer correctly to open it. Get three wrong and the maze keeps you.

  • Automatic progress. Finish a level and your stats update by themselves.
  • Adaptive. Do well and the doors get harder. Struggle and they ease off.
  • Hints. Two per puzzle, but every hint costs 5 points.
  • Explained. Every answer comes with the reasoning, right or wrong.
